# ScopeCareer Product Validation-1 — V3 Moderated Test Protocol

Status: Ready for initial ICP sessions  
Scope: category-defining interaction/value hypotheses only

## Participant target

Director/VP/C-level technology, digital, transformation, and operations leaders pursuing India/APAC/international opportunities.

Prefer participants with at least one senior-level transition or recruiter/executive-search interaction in the last three years.

## Session format

Target duration: **35–45 minutes**.

Do not explain internal terms such as `Pursuit Intelligence`, `Access Intelligence`, state-machine names, or the intended answer before behavioral observation.

Do not tell the participant that the five Pursuit Workspace areas are expected to remain stable across stages.

## Task 1 — Briefing and opportunity universe

Prompt:

> You have five minutes between meetings. Open this product and tell me what needs your attention in the next day.

Observe:

- whether Briefing is understood as an event/action ledger rather than a job list;
- whether the participant distinguishes change, due work, follow-up, and preparation;
- whether the participant reaches the relevant opportunity without searching again;
- whether generic dashboard metrics are requested before any meaningful action.

Then ask:

> Where would you go to see every opportunity you are currently considering?

Record as task `attention`, hypothesis `PV-H01`.

## Task 2 — Shortlist comparison

Prompt:

> Show me the roles on the shortlist. Pick two you are genuinely weighing and compare them.

Observe:

- discovery of Shortlist;
- discovery of compare selection;
- dimensions used first;
- whether the participant asks for one aggregate score;
- whether comparison changes a preference, research question, or next action.

Record as task `opportunity_compare`, hypothesis `PV-H06`.

## Task 3 — Opportunity decision dossier

Prompt:

> Open the VP Technology role. Explain whether it deserves more of your time and what could still change your mind.

Do not explain `Confirmed`, `Estimate`, `Open`, source trail, or route language unless the participant is blocked.

Observe whether the participant can identify:

- what is actually supported;
- source and recency of a material fact;
- what is estimated rather than confirmed;
- the highest-impact open question;
- career trajectory;
- relationship fact versus route interpretation.

Ask:

> Where did the claim that this reports to the CEO come from?

Failure signal: participant cannot trace a material claim to its source or treats an estimate/open question as fact.

Record as task `opportunity_dossier`, hypothesis `PV-H03`.

## Task 4 — Explore versus Pursue

Prompt:

> You are interested, but you are not ready to spend network capital yet. What would you do next?

After the contextual investigation action:

> You now want to treat this as a serious live opportunity. Show me what you would do.

Ask only after the action:

- What did the investigation action mean?
- What did `Open pursuit workspace` mean?
- Did either action imply applying, submitting, sharing, or contacting anyone?

Failure signal: either action is interpreted as an external effect.

Record as task `explore_pursue`, hypothesis `PV-H02`.

## Task 5 — Pursuit Workspace: next move

The participant should now be in the Pre-contact workspace.

Prompt:

> Without opening every section, tell me what you should do next, what you know now, and what is still blocking the decision.

Observe:

- time to identify the next move;
- whether `Brief` works as the default working file;
- whether current picture, open questions, route, readiness and recent activity are understandable;
- whether the participant expects this workspace to send anything automatically;
- whether five stable areas feel sufficient or whether the participant hunts for domain-specific tabs.

Then ask:

> If you closed the product now and came back later, what would you expect this workspace to remember for you?

Record as task `pursuit_workspace`, hypothesis `PV-H07`.

## Task 6 — Access route and evidence binding

Prompt A:

> Assume you do not want to click Apply. What is the strongest route into this company, and how confident are you in that route?

Observe distinction between:

- relationship fact;
- relationship interpretation;
- route recommendation;
- external action.

Prompt B:

> The role values international platform scaling. Show the system which part of the candidate's experience should support that mandate.

Ask:

> What changed when you selected that evidence? Was the claim verified, selected, or rewritten?

Correct conceptual interpretation should be close to: selected/bound for this mandate; evidence/truth status did not change.

Record as tasks `access_plan` / `evidence_binding`, hypotheses `PV-H04` / `PV-H05`.

## Task 7 — Stage-aware workspace

Use researcher controls or a direct stage URL. **Counterbalance across participants** instead of forcing every participant through every stage.

Recommended first-round allocation:

- P01–P02: Recruiter conversation
- P03–P04: Selection
- P05: Final / references
- P06: Offer
- P07–P08 if recruited: repeat the stage with the highest uncertainty from earlier sessions

Prompt:

> Time has moved forward. This opportunity is now at this stage. Tell me what changed, what matters most now, and what you would do next.

Observe:

- whether navigation remains understandable without explanation;
- whether the default Brief changes meaningfully with stage;
- whether People / Positioning / Process / Record reveal the right stage-specific context;
- whether irrelevant future modules dominate earlier stages;
- whether source/evidence revisions feel coherent when facts become known later.

For Recruiter stage, inspect debrief/commitments.  
For Selection stage, inspect next stakeholder / known concern / evidence.  
For Final stage, inspect case/reference work.  
For Offer stage, inspect terms/trade-offs and confirm that offer intent is not external acceptance.

Record as task `stage_aware_workspace`, hypothesis `PV-H08`.

## Optional Task 8 — Mobile companion check

Use only when the session format permits a second device/viewport.

Prompt:

> On your phone, open Opportunities and inspect one role.

Observe whether the user experiences a clear list → detail flow rather than a long stacked desktop workspace.

This task is currently supporting evidence, not a separate P0 hypothesis.

## Post-task interview

Ask only after behavioral tasks:

1. What would make this part of a real executive-search routine rather than another job tracker?
2. Which part felt most differentiated?
3. Which labels/actions felt unnatural or unclear?
4. Where did the product ask for too much interpretation or too much data?
5. Which decision still required leaving the product?
6. At what point did the workspace become too heavy, if at all?
7. Would this remain useful when not actively job searching? Why?

## Record per participant

- participant segment/current seniority;
- recent search experience;
- task completion;
- time to first meaningful action;
- terminology confusion;
- spontaneous value statements;
- trust concerns;
- missing information;
- requested features as observations, not automatic scope;
- hypothesis outcome: supported / mixed / contradicted / not tested;
- qualitative confidence.

## Scope recut gate

Do not move to production technical architecture merely because V3 is usable.

Before the V1 scope recut, synthesize at least:

- Briefing versus Opportunities comprehension;
- dossier/provenance comprehension;
- Explore versus Pursue commitment comprehension;
- value and credibility of Access guidance;
- evidence-binding value/friction;
- comparison usefulness;
- whether the default Pursuit Brief exposes the next move fast enough;
- whether stage-aware workspace composition is useful without becoming enterprise-dashboard complexity;
- which terms/interactions require redesign.

Validation may change V1 surface design. It must not casually remove provenance, assertion/evidence status, trust-plane separation, external-effect separation, or candidate authority.
