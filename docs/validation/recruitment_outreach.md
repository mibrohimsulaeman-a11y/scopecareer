# ScopeCareer Product Validation-1 — Recruitment Outreach

Status: Copy-ready; sending requires explicit recipient choice

## Short DM / WhatsApp

Hi — saya sedang menguji prototype produk career intelligence untuk Director/VP/C-level, khususnya technology/digital/transformation/operations. Fokusnya bukan job board atau resume builder, tetapi bagaimana senior leaders memilih opportunity yang layak dikejar, menilai access route, dan memakai evidence karier secara lebih terstruktur.

Saya mencari beberapa orang untuk sesi research 25–35 menit. Ini prototype dengan data fiktif, tidak ada job application atau outreach yang benar-benar dikirim. Yang diuji adalah apakah konsep dan interaction-nya masuk akal untuk executive search.

Kalau bersedia, saya akan kirim beberapa screening question singkat dan jadwal yang cocok.

## Formal email / LinkedIn message

Subject: 30-minute research session — executive career intelligence prototype

I’m running a small product-research round for an executive career intelligence prototype designed for Director, VP, and C-level leaders in technology, digital, transformation, and operations.

The research is focused on how senior professionals decide which opportunities deserve attention, whether a role is a good career move rather than merely a skills match, how they think about recruiter/referral access, and how career evidence should support positioning.

The session takes approximately 25–35 minutes and uses fictional candidate/company/opportunity data. It is not a live job-search service and no employer, recruiter, or job application is contacted or submitted during the session.

If you’ve had a senior-level career transition or recruiter/executive-search interaction in the last few years and are open to participating, I can send a short screener and scheduling options.

## Research integrity

Do not tell recruits:

- that route-to-company guidance is expected to be a differentiator;
- that the contextual investigation/preparation actions and `Open pursuit workspace` are intended to represent different commitment levels;
- that the interaction is inspired by dating apps;
- which hypothesis the participant is expected to validate.

Recruitment copy should describe the domain, not teach the intended answer.
