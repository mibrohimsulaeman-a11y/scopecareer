# ScopeCareer Product Validation-1 — Participant Screener

Status: Ready for recruitment  
Last updated: 2026-08-21

## Purpose

Recruit participants who can evaluate ScopeCareer's category-defining candidate experience. This screener is not intended to statistically represent the entire executive market.

## Target sample

Initial round: **6–8 moderated sessions**.

Target mix:

- Director / VP / C-level technology, digital, transformation, or operations leaders;
- India, Singapore/APAC, or executives who have actively considered international roles;
- at least 4 participants with a senior-level transition in the last 3 years;
- at least 3 participants with recruiter or executive-search-consultant interaction;
- at least 2 participants who have considered confidential/non-public opportunities;
- include both currently employed and actively searching participants where possible.

Do not recruit only highly technical AI enthusiasts. MCP familiarity is not required for Product Validation-1.

## Screening questions

1. What is your current or most recent seniority?
   - Senior Manager
   - Director
   - VP / Head of Function
   - C-level
   - Other

2. Which function best describes your work?
   - Technology / Engineering
   - Digital
   - Transformation
   - Operations
   - Product
   - Other

3. In the last 3 years, have you considered or completed a senior-level job transition?
   - Yes, completed
   - Yes, actively considered / searched
   - No

4. In the last 3 years, which channels have surfaced serious career opportunities for you? Select all that apply.
   - Public job posting
   - Internal recruiter
   - External recruiter
   - Retained executive-search consultant
   - Referral / former colleague
   - Direct company relationship
   - Board/adviser/investor network
   - Other

5. Have you discussed a role that did not have a public job description or public application URL?
   - Yes
   - No

6. Have you considered roles outside your current country in the last 3 years?
   - Yes
   - No

7. Which statement best describes your current career-search state?
   - Not actively searching, but open to the right opportunity
   - Quietly exploring
   - Actively searching
   - In an active selection process
   - Recently completed a transition

8. Are you willing to participate in a 25–35 minute screen-shared product research session?
   - Yes
   - No

## Exclusion / deprioritisation

Deprioritise for the first round when:

- participant has no senior-level search/recruiter experience and cannot reason about executive opportunities;
- participant is evaluating only entry-level/high-volume job search;
- participant helped design ScopeCareer or has already been taught the intended meaning of the prototype's research/pursuit actions or recommended access route;
- participant cannot give informed consent for the session.

## Research disclosure

Tell participants:

- this is a prototype, not a live job-search service;
- opportunity/company/candidate examples in the prototype are fictional research fixtures;
- actions do not apply to jobs or contact employers;
- the goal is to test comprehension and decision value, not the participant's career performance;
- recording, if any, requires separate consent.
