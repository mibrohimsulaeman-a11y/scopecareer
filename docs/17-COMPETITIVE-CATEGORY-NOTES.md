# ScopeCareer — Competitive and Category Notes

Status: Research-backed working notes; not a formal competitor report
Last updated: 2026-08-21

## 1. Category problem

A candidate product limited to:

`Career Profile → Job Discovery → Match → Resume → Interview Prep → Tracker`

risks landing inside the existing AI job-search category.

The differentiation target is:

`Career Evidence → Market Intelligence → Opportunity Quality → Pursuit Intelligence → Access Intelligence → Executive Positioning → Selection Intelligence → Outcome Learning`

## 2. Commodity envelope to assume

Modern job-search products may already offer substantial combinations of:

- master resume/profile;
- job matching;
- resume tailoring;
- application tracking;
- interview preparation;
- browser capture;
- contact tracking;
- alerts;
- mobile access;
- referral/insider hints.

Therefore these features are necessary workflow components, not sufficient category differentiation.

Exact competitor capability claims must be re-verified before external presentations.

## 3. ScopeCareer differentiation hypotheses

### Executive Career Evidence Graph

Deeper than a CV-shaped skill list: mandate, business scale, P&L/budget, organisation/geography, board exposure, transformation context, decisions, and evidence status.

### Pursuit Intelligence

Separates `Can I do it?` from `Should I spend career/network capital on it?`

### Opportunity Quality

Assesses role/company/source quality independently from candidate fit.

### Access Intelligence

Treats recruiter/search-consultant/referral/network route as first-class rather than assuming direct apply.

### Confidential Opportunity Model

Opportunity exists without public vacancy/JD.

### Selection Process Intelligence

Models multi-stakeholder executive selection, debriefs, concerns, and unresolved questions rather than only generating interview questions.

### Agent-access substrate

Candidate can bring trusted career intelligence to supported AI clients through MCP without copying the entire private career graph into ad hoc prompts.

### Progressive interaction

Lightweight Daily Shortlist and Pass/Watch/Explore/Pursue instead of dense dashboard-first UX.

## 4. Executive-search practice hypothesis

The product assumes senior/executive search places materially more weight on:

- search consultants;
- relationship building;
- warm introductions;
- confidential/recruiter-led mandates;
- target-company strategy;
- role mandate/scope;
- multi-stakeholder selection.

This direction is supported by public executive-search guidance discussed during concept development, but a formal market-research pack should verify practices by segment/geography.

## 6. Positioning guardrail

Do not position as:

- "Tinder for Jobs";
- "AI that applies to hundreds of jobs";
- "100% accurate executive match";
- "autonomous career agent".

Preferred category language:

**Personal Executive Career Intelligence System**

Preferred short promise:

**Know where to play. Know what to pursue. Know how to get in.**
